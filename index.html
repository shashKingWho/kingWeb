Experimental gameplay systems, game jams, interaction design, rapid prototyping, and creative mechanics developed under the shashKing label.

shashKing (Shashaank Singh in the credits) is an indie game developer, technical product manager, XR architect, and multimedia creator. Most releases start as browser-based or experimental HTML5 games on itch.io: Gerabill Sleeps (submitted to GMTK Game Jam 2026) and Chamkila Dhaba among them, alongside mobile AR games like Bats and Wolves and Shera, and the puzzle adventure Dinosaur Day.

Right now, almost everything else is on pause. After watching Dhurandhar Part 1, shashKing got (by his own admission) a little too devoted to one project: Project Lyari, a GTA-scale gang-war simulation set in Lyari. Other work, AR experiments included, has mostly stopped. The only exceptions have been a couple of game jams taken purely for fresh creative juice, mainly to promote his own QWERTY Game Genre (the K Board concept), before diving straight back into Lyari's streets.

DO YOU POPCORN

A chaotic browser game where your job is to help your boss make popcorn using a shotgun. Time your shots carefully, manage reloads, and avoid missing kernels. Built around a dedicated reload mechanic that keeps every second tense and ridiculous.

PROJECT LYARI

A top-down action game set in Lyari, Pakistan, where you command a covert black-ops network across gang wars and political corruption. Lead operations, hold territory, and keep the city's enemies stuck in a loop of violence and revenge.



shashKing (Shashaank Singh) is an indie game developer, technical product manager, XR architect, and multimedia creator.

Key Details & Work

• Indie Games: Creates browser-based and experimental HTML5 games on shashKing itch.io, including titles like Gerabill Sleeps (submitted to GMTK Game Jam 2026), Chamkila Dhaba, Deez Gunz, and Dinosaur Day.
• XR & Tech Background: Works as a technical product manager and XR architect focusing on interactive web development, game design, and immersive realities.
• Content Creation: Shares devlogs, game design insights, and creative projects on the Planet shashKing YouTube channel and shashKing X (Twitter).
• Music: Has released songs and tracks such as "Shehzadi", "Aaj Khane me kya bana hai", and others on platforms like Spotify.

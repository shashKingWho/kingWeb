XR FUNDAMENTALS CHALLENGE

Built by shashKing for the Games for Change initiative, XR Fundamentals treats immersive education as an interactive challenge. As spatial computing rapidly evolves, understanding how we interact with digital space (beyond flat 2D screens) is critical for designers, developers, and digital citizens alike. Answer a curated, randomized series of questions on spatial understanding, XR-assisted maintenance, and on-body interaction design: score 70% or higher to pass and unlock your spatial literacy breakdown.

"Without physical resistance, mid-air interactions feel floaty and imprecise... By utilizing tactile feedback directly on the skin via headset hand tracking, we can bring true immersion back to spatial computing." — shashKing, on the Invisible Button Study
